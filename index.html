How the board works
  • Double-click the board, or press Add note, to pin a note.
  • To connect two notes, press Link on one, then click the other. Dragging the red pin onto another note works too.
  • Click a string to label it or cut it. Press N for a new note.
  • Drop or paste images onto the board. Mode switches between dragging the board and box-selecting cards. Ctrl/Cmd+A selects everything, and selected cards move together.
